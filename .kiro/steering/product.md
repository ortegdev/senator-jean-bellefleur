# Product Vision: Diaspora4Bellefleur

## Mission

A transparent, multilingual platform enabling the Haitian diaspora to learn about Jean Fritznel Bellefleur's candidacy for Senator of the Nord-Ouest department and make small-dollar contributions to support his campaign.

## Core Principles

### Transparency First
- Clear affiliation disclosure on every page
- Verified candidacy status always visible
- Source citations for all factual claims
- Open about what we know and don't know

### Diaspora-Centric
- Multilingual by default (Haitian Creole, French, English minimum)
- Accessible from diaspora population centers (US, Canada, France, etc.)
- Currency and payment methods familiar to diaspora
- Content that connects department issues to diaspora interests

### Small-Dollar Focus
- Low barriers to entry (minimum contributions)
- Cumulative caps to encourage broad participation
- Clear, simple contribution flows
- Compliance-first approach to protect donors and candidate

### Isolation & Independence
- Separate from any other candidate or campaign sites
- Independent infrastructure, database, and accounts
- Own domain and branding
- No cross-linking to other campaigns

## Target Users

### Primary: Haitian Diaspora Members
- Living in US, Canada, France, Caribbean
- Ties to Nord-Ouest department (family, hometown)
- Want to support fair representation
- May have limited time to research

### Secondary: Nord-Ouest Residents  
- Want to learn about candidates
- May not be able to contribute (legal restrictions)
- Share info with diaspora family

## Key Features (Planned)

### Phase 1: Information
- Candidate profile (sourced, verified)
- Department information (communes, issues)
- Candidacy status tracker
- Multilingual content

### Phase 2: Engagement
- Email updates (opt-in)
- Social sharing tools
- FAQ section
- Contact form

### Phase 3: Contributions (Gated)
- Secure donation flow
- Compliance checks
- Receipt generation
- Contribution tracking

### Phase 4: Community
- Supporter stories (opt-in, moderated)
- Volunteer signup
- Event calendar
- Progress updates

## Success Metrics

- Visitor engagement (time on site, pages viewed)
- Email list growth
- Geographic reach (diaspora locations)
- Contribution participation rate
- Contribution average (should stay in small-dollar range)
- Zero compliance violations
- Zero privacy breaches

## What This Platform Is NOT

- ❌ Not an official campaign website
- ❌ Not a news or media organization
- ❌ Not a political party site
- ❌ Not a general advocacy platform
- ❌ Not connected to any other campaign or candidate

## Content Guidelines

### Always Include
- Source for factual claims
- Date of information
- Verification status
- Context for data points

### Never Include  
- Unverified claims
- Fabricated testimonials
- Unofficial endorsements
- Private personal information
- Speculation presented as fact

## Localization Strategy

### Language Priority
1. **Haitian Creole (ht)** - Primary
2. **French (fr)** - Secondary
3. **English (en)** - Tertiary

### Content Adaptation
- Not just translation - cultural adaptation
- Department-specific terminology
- Diaspora-relevant framing
- Respect for Haitian political culture

## Risk Management

### Compliance Risks
- **Mitigation**: Database-driven rules, legal review requirement, conservative limits

### Privacy Risks  
- **Mitigation**: Minimal data collection, encryption, no public donor lists default

### Verification Risks
- **Mitigation**: Required sources, content review workflow, clear "unverified" labels

### Technical Risks
- **Mitigation**: Separate infrastructure, regular backups, security audits

## Future Considerations

- Integration with official campaign (if authorization obtained)
- Volunteer coordination tools
- Event ticketing
- Matching contribution campaigns
- Impact reports for donors
